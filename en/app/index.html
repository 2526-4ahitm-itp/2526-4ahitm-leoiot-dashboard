App

The LeoIoT app

Room climate of the connected rooms and photovoltaic data of the school in one place: a 3D model of the building, a dashboard, and a kiosk display for screens in the building.

Currently one sensor box delivers real readings (room 105); the other rooms are simulated.

Technical documentationSee the architecture

3D explorer

The school building as an interactive 3D model. Click a room to see its values.

  • Rotating and zooming the building; switching between basement, ground floor, floor 1, floor 2 and the gym; resetting the view.
  • Room selection by click: the room is highlighted and a panel shows its name, temperature and, where data exists, CO₂. Values update live while the room is selected.
  • Heatmaps for temperature (°C) and CO₂ (ppm) per floor, with a value label on each room. Available in single-floor views.
  • Navigation: room search with suggestions while typing; an animated path leads from the entrance to the room, also across floors and to the gym.

Dashboard: room climate

Temperature and CO₂ of all rooms, as current values, history and a table.

  • Room selection: a single room or all rooms; search by room name or filter by floor (labels as in the app: “All”, “Floor 1”, “Floor 2”, “Floor E”, “Keller”).
  • Summary cards for the current temperature and CO₂ and for the 24-hour averages.
  • Charts for temperature and CO₂ over time, with a selectable time range: 1 h, 6 h, 24 h, 7 days, or a single day (with previous/next buttons and a date picker).
  • Table of all sensors with time of the last update and a status (OK, Medium, Alert). Only a few rooms are shown at first; a click shows more.
  • Values update live without reloading the page. The language can be switched between German and English.

Photovoltaics

Energy data of the school PV system, as a separate view of the dashboard.

  • Cards: yield today, total yield, battery (currently charging or discharging) and power grid (power is drawn from or sent to the grid).
  • Charts that add up solar yield and the building’s power use over time (measured in kWh = kilowatt-hours).
  • Daily statistics: stored in battery, taken from battery, bought from grid, fed into grid.
  • The same day selection as for the room data, so past days can be viewed. For the current day the view refreshes every 30 seconds.

Kiosk

A full-screen energy display for screens in the school building (interface in German only).

  • Header with HTL logo, clock and time of the last update; light and dark mode switch.
  • Two donut charts for today: consumption (from PV, from grid, from battery) and production (self-consumption, to grid, to battery).
  • Weekly overview of the last 7 days (produced, consumed, exported) and the power curve of today in kW for production and consumption.
  • Live energy flow between solar, consumption, grid and battery, with animated lines. Live values arrive continuously; charts reload every 5 minutes.

Traffic-light colours in the dashboard

Status is always shown as a word as well, never by colour alone. The dashboard table rates each room like this:

  • OKCO₂ up to 800 ppm (parts per million, a measure of CO₂ in the air) and temperature between 19 and 24 °C
  • MediumCO₂ above 800 up to 1000 ppm
  • AlertCO₂ above 1000 ppm, or temperature outside 19 to 24 °C

The 3D model instead colours rooms on a smooth scale (a heatmap): from green (about 400 ppm) through yellow and orange (800 to 1000 ppm) to red (from 1700 ppm).
