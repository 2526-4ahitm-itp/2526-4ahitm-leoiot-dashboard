School project at HTL Leonding

How good is the air in our school?

LeoIoT shows temperature and CO₂ of the connected rooms live in a 3D model of the school building – together with data from the school’s photovoltaic (solar power) system.

Features

Functional scope

Four functions: 3D model, room climate dashboard, PV view and kiosk display – to make the school’s room climate and energy understandable.

3D model of the school

Rotate the building and view it floor by floor. Rooms are colour-coded directly in the model.

Live sensor data

Temperature and CO₂ of the connected rooms. A traffic-light colour and a word (OK, Medium, Alert) show how good the air is.

Technology

How it works

From the measuring point in the classroom to the browser display in four steps. PV data comes separately from the Solax Cloud (the manufacturer’s portal of the PV system).

  1. 1

    Sensor box

    Small measuring computer (ESP32)

    Measures temperature and CO₂ in the room.

  2. 2

    MQTT

    Message relay

    Receives the readings and passes them on to the database and the app.

  3. 3

    InfluxDB

    Database for measurements over time

    Stores every reading with its time, so the history can be viewed.

  4. 4

    App

    Web app in the browser

    Shows rooms, traffic lights and PV data in the 3D explorer and the dashboard.
